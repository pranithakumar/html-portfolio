about the movie

Dev is a 2019 Tamil action-adventure romance film starring Karthi and Rakul Preet Singh.
directed by Rajath Ravishankar, about an adrenaline-junkie from a rich family (Dev) who falls for a career-focused businesswoman (Meghna)
leading him on a journey to win her over through grand adventures, blending action, travel, romance.
mixed critical reception for its weak story despite visuals.
